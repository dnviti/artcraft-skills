## 1.0.0 (2026-10-28)

### Features

* initial release of the ArtCraft skills suite, 14 skills:
  * `figcraft-design` (FigCraft: UI design and design-to-code)
  * `wordcraft-documents` (WordCraft: word processing)
  * `deckcraft-presentations` (DeckCraft: slide decks)
  * `gridcraft-spreadsheets` (GridCraft: spreadsheets)
  * `pdfcraft-documents` (PdfCraft: PDF editing, forms, redaction, signing)
  * `photocraft-images` (PhotoCraft: raster image editing)
  * `vectorcraft-illustration` (VectorCraft: vector illustration)
  * `solidcraft-cad` (SolidCraft: parametric 3D CAD)
  * `cadcraft-drawings` (CadCraft: 2D technical drawings)
  * `designcraft-layout` (DesignCraft: multi-page print layout)
  * `effectcraft-motion` (EffectCraft: motion graphics and compositing)
  * `filmcraft-video` (FilmCraft: video editing and grading)
  * `lightcraft-photos` (LightCraft: photo library and raw development)
  * `soundcraft-audio` (SoundCraft: audio and MIDI)
